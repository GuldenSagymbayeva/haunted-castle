# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-02

First public release.

- 11-room castle with locked doors, a hidden passage and a final boss.
- Fighter and Mage heroes; Slime, Skeleton, Ghost, Werewolf and Vampire monsters, each with its own rule.
- Potions, weapons, armour, throwable holy water, keys, tome and telescope.
- Save and continue, three difficulty levels, score and rank, `hint` command.
- Colour output with health bars.
- Custom worlds from text files, with errors reported by line number.
- Maven build, 37 JUnit 5 tests including Monte Carlo balance checks, CI on Java 17 and 21,
  and automatic release of a runnable jar.
