DESC entrance_hall Moonlight spills through a shattered rose window onto a cracked marble floor. The great doors will not budge.
DESC great_hall A long banquet table is still laid for a feast that ended centuries ago. Portraits follow you with their eyes.
DESC kitchen Copper pots swing gently with no wind to move them. A pot of stew is somehow still warm.
DESC chapel Candles burn on a dusty altar. For the first time tonight you feel safe.
DESC armory Racks of rusted weapons line the walls. The floor is covered in deep claw marks.
DESC library Towering shelves sag under rotting books. A cold draft seems to come from behind one bookcase.
DESC tower A winding stair ends in a round chamber open to the stars. Your breath turns to frost.
DESC laboratory Bubbling glassware and scorched tables. Something wet glistens between the shelves.
DESC dungeon Chains hang from dripping walls. A heavy iron gate, carved with a skull, leads down to the crypt.
DESC secret_passage A narrow, cobwebbed tunnel slopes steeply downward. You hear something breathing far below.
DESC crypt Rows of stone coffins. The largest one stands open... and empty.

DESC Ooze A quivering green mass. The bigger it is, the harder it hits.
DESC Old_Bones A skeleton warrior. Smashed bones have a way of pulling themselves back together.
DESC Lady_Grey A weeping spirit. Ordinary blows often pass right through her.
DESC Grimfang A huge grey wolf-man. Wounding it only makes it angrier, but silver burns it.
DESC Count_Orlok The master of the castle. He feeds on blood, and only silver or magic can finish him.
